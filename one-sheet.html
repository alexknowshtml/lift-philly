Tax Reform

Solution-focused, not political. We're all Philadelphians solving a problem together.

The problem is in the system, not individuals. No blame, no shame. The system was designed for a different economy.

Shepherd mindset. Shepherds focus on solutions with love. Be the shepherd when advocating for LIFT. In the room with us? Share your fear, uncertainty, and doubt—we'll help you through it.

The Problem

Philadelphia's two-class tax system (Corporations vs. Unincorporated) was designed for a W-2 economy. That's not how Philadelphia—or the world—works anymore.

122,000
Philadelphians—85% of all city businesses—are nonemployer firms: contractors, freelancers, gig workers, home daycares, barbers, food truck vendors, solo practitioners. They now face massive tax increases they cannot afford.

What does it mean for a family earning $50,000/year?

Families earning just $50K face a NEW tax bill increase of:

$1,208 – $3,236 in April 2026

Translation: 1-3 months rent. Monthly groceries for a family of 4. A winter heating bill.

Know Before You File liftphilly.org/calculator

This is the most diverse class of business—by race, by category, by size. For many, self-employment is about survival and dignity. This is our city's equity issue.

The Solution

Create a third tax class for "Individuals and Single-Member LLCs whose sole member is an Individual"—ending double taxation on Philadelphia's small business economy.

What Changes

  • ✓ Exempt from BIRT Net Income Tax (5.71%) — this is the double taxation
  • ✓ Exempt from BIRT Gross Receipts Tax (0.141%)
  • ✓ Still pay Net Profits Tax (3.74%) — only for Philadelphia residents

Result: $1,208 becomes $0. $3,236 becomes $0. (100% reduction)

Already confirmed: Solution has been reviewed and confirmed constitutional by Philadelphia Law Department. Critical that any solution passes the uniformity clause.
