Personalize your email

3
Preview your email

Here's a sample email you can send. You'll be able to edit it in the next step below.

To:
CC:
Subject:Need your support for Bill 251026
Dear [COUNCIL MEMBER NAME]: My name is [YOUR NAME], a constituent in your district. I am writing in support of Bill 251026, introduced by Councilman Mike Driscoll and sponsored by 8 other council members. I would like you to publicly support a finance hearing this fall. [1-3 sentences about how this year's BIRT rollback impacted you and our community directly] I know I'm not alone in this request. I'm one of over 1,150 Philadelphians who signed in support of the LIFT act, because we're taxed higher than corporations, on the same income, twice. I need you to get Bill 251026 done before we're forced out of our businesses and homes. Thank you, [Your Name]

4
Send your email

Complete steps 1–3 above and your send options will appear here.

If that doesn't work, use the Copy buttons above to paste each piece manually.

Want to make an even bigger impact?

Call the office of each person you just emailed and leave a voicemail with the same message. For elected officials, voicemails carry more weight than emails — and emails carry more weight than social media comments.
