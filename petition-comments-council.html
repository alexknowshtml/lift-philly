Constituent Record
Submitted to Philadelphia City Council — May 6, 2026 Hearing on Bill 251026

The following 210 statements were submitted by Philadelphia residents and business owners who signed the LIFT Philly petition at liftphilly.org. These are their words, unedited, explaining how the elimination of the $100,000 BIRT exemption has affected their lives and livelihoods. Signers represent every corner of the city’s independent workforce — from therapists and artists to landlords, freelancers, and community supporters — united by a shared call for relief.
